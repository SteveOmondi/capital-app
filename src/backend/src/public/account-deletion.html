3 Ways to Delete Your Account

01

In-App Deletion

Delete your profile directly from within the 98.4 Capital FM Android or iOS mobile application:

  1. Open the Capital FM App.
  2. Tap on Profile / Settings tab.
  3. Select Account & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.
02

Web Self-Service

Use our direct web form above on this page to trigger instantaneous profile deletion via our backend gateway.

  1. Enter your registered email address.
  2. Confirm deletion in the prompt.
  3. Your record is deleted immediately.
03

Email Support Request

Contact our dedicated Data Privacy Team to process your request manually:

  1. Send email to privacy@capitalfm.co.ke
  2. Subject: Account Deletion Request
  3. Processed within 48 business hours.
